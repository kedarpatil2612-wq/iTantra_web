EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CHALLENGE

Bridging Communication Gaps in Extreme Tactical Terrains

Conventional tactical radios struggle with severe bandwidth constraints (1.2 - 4.8 kbps), heavy atmospheric noise, and India's vast linguistic diversity. iTantra solves this via edge neural vocoding and multilingual STT/TTS synthesis.
